About this project

Health Icons is a volunteer effort to create a ‘global good’ for health projects all over the world. These icons are available in the public domain for use in any type of project.

The Health Icons project was started in 2021 during the Covid 19 pandemic. A group of volunteers originally came together to upgrade the icons used in the open source DHIS2 health software project. This volunteer effort expanded into the Health Icons project to provide high quality free-to-use icons for the entire world. Read our full history here.

The project is hosted by the public health not-for-profit Resolve to Save Lives as an expression of our commitment to offer the icons for free, forever.

Need an icon for your project? Submit a request and we will do our best to respond. Want to help make icons? Here's how to contribute.

Packages and Figma plugin

Using Health Icons in your website or app? Our packages for React, Vue, Svelte and plain JavaScript let you import just the icons you need, in either style, at any size and in any color. Designing in Figma? The Health Icons Figma plugin lets you find icons and add them to your designs without leaving Figma.

Credit & inspiration

Some of these icons are based on open source icons in DHIS2 and from Medic.org. We also took inspiration from the excellent open source Remix Icon collection. Thanks!
